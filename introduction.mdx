Leantime is a goals-focused project management system that brings strategy, planning, and task execution together in one place. Unlike traditional tools built for professional project managers, Leantime is designed to be approachable for everyone on your team — built with neurodiversity in mind to support people with ADHD, dyslexia, and autism.

Whether you're running sprints like Jira, organizing work like Trello, or tracking strategic goals like a full-featured platform, Leantime gives you the tools without the complexity.
