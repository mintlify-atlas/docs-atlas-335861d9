---
title: "Log and report time with Leantime timesheets"
description: "Track time on tasks using a timer or manual entry, review weekly timesheets, and export reports filtered by user, project, or date range."
---

Leantime's time tracking is built directly into your tasks. You can start a timer while you work, or log hours manually after the fact. The weekly timesheet view gives you and your team a complete picture of where time went, and reports let you slice that data by user, project, or date range.

## Logging time on a task

There are two ways to record time against a task:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Timer">
    Open any task and click **Start timer**. The timer runs in the background — you can navigate away and it will keep counting. When you're done, click **Stop timer**. The elapsed time is recorded as a new entry on that task for today.

    Only one timer can run at a time. Starting a timer on a second task automatically stops the first.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Manual entry">
    Open any task and click **Log time**. Enter the number of hours, select the date, and choose a work type (see below). Click **Save** to record the entry.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Work types

Each time entry is categorized by work type. The available types are:

- **General billable**
- **General not billable**
- **Project management**
- **Development**
- **Bug fixing (not billable)**
- **Testing**

Work types appear in reports and can be used to filter by billable vs. non-billable hours.

## Weekly timesheet view

The weekly timesheet view shows a matrix of tasks on the vertical axis and days of the week on the horizontal axis. Each cell contains the hours logged for that task on that day.

To open your timesheet, click **Timesheets** in the main navigation. The current week is shown by default. Use the arrow buttons to move to previous or future weeks.

You can edit any cell directly in the timesheet view to adjust logged hours without opening the individual task.

<Note>
  Managers and admins can switch the user selector at the top of the timesheet view to review another team member's hours.
</Note>

## Timesheet reports

The **Reports** view aggregates time entries and lets you filter by:

- **User** — see one person's hours or all users side by side
- **Project** — narrow to a single project or view across all projects
- **Date range** — pick any custom range

Reports show totals by day, week, or month, broken down by task and work type.

### Exporting data

Click **Export** in the reports view to download timesheet data as a CSV file. The export includes the task name, project, date, hours, work type, and the user who logged the time.

## Role-based visibility

What you can see in timesheets depends on your role:

| Role | Can see |
|------|---------|
| Regular user | Own timesheets only |
| Manager | Own timesheets and all users in their projects |
| Admin / Owner | All timesheets across all projects |

<Tip>
  If you need to correct a time entry you logged on the wrong task or date, open the task, find the entry in the time log section, and click the edit icon next to it.
</Tip>
